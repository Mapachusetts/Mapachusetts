            About Mapachusetts

            Mapachusetts is a civic transparency tool built to make Massachusetts political information accessible to everyone — regardless of how closely you follow the news. We pull from public records, campaign finance filings, and legislative data to give you a clear, unbiased picture of who represents you and how they act on it.
